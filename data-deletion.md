# Data Deletion Instructions for AgentChat

_Last updated: October 15, 2025_

Thank you for using **AgentChat**.

---

## 1. Does AgentChat Store Personal Data?
AgentChat does **not store or retain any personal data**.  
All user interactions and messages are processed temporarily for providing responses and are not saved on our servers.

---

## 2. How to Request Data Deletion
Since AgentChat does not collect or store any user data, there is **no data to delete**.  
However, if you believe your information has been collected through a third-party platform (such as GitHub, chat APIs, or hosting services), please follow these steps:

1. Identify the platform or service through which you used AgentChat.  
2. Contact that platform directly to request deletion of your data.  
3. If you need assistance, you may contact us at:

## 3. Third-Party Data
If AgentChat integrates with any external services or APIs in the future, data deletion procedures for those services will be provided and linked here.
